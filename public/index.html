Priority 5 = critical · 1 = whenever.“Assign to” locks a chore to one person; “Auto” lets the scheduler balance it.

Cadence drives when a chore’s obligation opens. Once open, the holder has the grace window to check it off; miss it and it goes delinquent — pinned to the same person at the top of their day. After the set number of delinquency cycles, an auto-assigned chore escalates to an adult. Checking it off stamps Last done and starts the next cycle.

Apply matrix creates one chore per enabled room × type cell (e.g. Mop — Kitchen), each inheriting the type’s characteristics with minutes scaled by room size. It preserves the completion history and per-room assignee of chores that already exist, adds new combinations, and removes chores for cells you’ve unchecked or rooms/types you’ve deleted.
